Our Mission

Our Singam Live & Life Care Centre is a home that providing the best nursing services to the aged, sickly and rehabilitation for the needy. Singam Live & Life Care Centre is basically a care centre for adults

Singam Live & Life Care Centre has been running over 25 years staring from 1995. We helping people with poor income, bedridden and others who need helps. Our care centre officially registered on 3rd January 1998. Starting from that, we provide catering for the need with various cases such as stroke, cancer, psychiatric, accident case and other cases that we can help with staff working round the clock.
